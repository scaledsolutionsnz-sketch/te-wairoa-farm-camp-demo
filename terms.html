The ground rules for staying at Te Wairoa Farm Camp.

1. About these terms

These terms apply to anyone staying at Te Wairoa Farm Camp at 1312 Sandhills Road, Ahipara, Northland, and to anyone using this website. By camping with us you agree to them.

2. Site fees

Site fees are charged per person, per night, and are payable on site. Rates shown on this website are a guide and may change. The rate you are quoted when you ring is the rate that applies to your stay.

3. Bookings and availability

We do not run an online booking system. Availability is confirmed by phone or email only. Ringing ahead is strongly recommended, particularly over summer and public holidays. We cannot guarantee a site to anyone who arrives without contacting us first.

4. Seasonal opening

The camping area is part of a working beef farm and is only open to campers during the summer months, when the beach frontage is out of the farm rotation. Opening and closing dates vary from year to year and are at our discretion.

5. Facilities

Facilities are basic and are described honestly on this website. There is no mains power, no flush toilets and no hot water. Campers are expected to be self sufficient for the length of their stay. Facilities may be unavailable from time to time for maintenance or for reasons outside our control.

6. Farm safety

You are camping on a working farm. Gates must be closed behind you, farm vehicles and machinery have right of way, and livestock must not be approached, fed or disturbed. Children are the responsibility of their parents or caregivers at all times.

7. Dogs and other animals

Dogs are welcome but must be under control at all times and kept on a lead anywhere near stock. Owners are responsible for their dog's behaviour and for cleaning up after it. Any dog that worries stock will be asked to leave, along with its owner.

8. Beach and water safety

Ninety Mile Beach is an open west coast surf beach with strong currents and no lifeguard service. Swimming, surfcasting, driving on the beach and all other beach activities are undertaken entirely at your own risk.

9. Behaviour

We ask campers to keep noise down after dark, take rubbish to the bins provided, respect other campers and leave their site as they found it. We may ask anyone who does not to leave without a refund.

10. Fires

Open fires are subject to seasonal fire restrictions and to our permission on the day. Always check with us before lighting anything, and check the current fire season status for the Far North district.

11. Liability

Camping here is at your own risk. To the extent permitted by law, Te Wairoa Farm Camp is not liable for loss, theft or damage to vehicles, tents, equipment or personal belongings, or for injury or illness, except where caused by our negligence. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 or the Fair Trading Act 1986.

12. Changes

We may update these terms from time to time. The version published on this website is the one that applies.

13. Contact

Questions about these terms can go to Mandy on 021 223 1194, or use the email button on the contact section of the home page.
