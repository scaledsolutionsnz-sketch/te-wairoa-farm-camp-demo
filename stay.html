The facilities

Basic, and we say so up front

This is a farm paddock that opens up for the summer, not a holiday park. What is here is here, and it does the job.

  • Portaloo toilets, stocked with paper
  • Outdoor showers, cold water
  • Outdoor kitchen area and rubbish bins
  • Drinking water on site
  • Beach access straight off the property

No power, no flush toilets, no hot showers. If that is what you are after, there are holiday parks back in Ahipara township.

Worth knowing

Bring this, and shut the gates

  • Bring your own cooking gear, water containers and everything you need to be self sufficient for the stay.
  • Cash for the site fee is easiest. Ring ahead if that does not suit and Mandy will sort something.
  • It is a working farm. Shut every gate behind you and give the stock a wide berth.
  • Dogs are welcome, on a lead near the stock and under control on the beach.
  • Mobile coverage out here is patchy. Sort your plans before you leave town.
  • Take your rubbish to the bins and leave the paddock the way you found it.
