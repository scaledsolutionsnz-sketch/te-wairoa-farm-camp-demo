Privacy Policy

What we do with the details you give us when you get in touch about camping.

Placeholder wording for this demo. Te Wairoa Farm Camp should review and adjust it to match how the camp actually handles camper details before the site goes live.

1. Who this covers

This policy covers Te Wairoa Farm Camp, 1312 Sandhills Road, Ahipara, Northland, and this website. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

Only what we need to hold a site for you. Typically that is your name, a contact phone number or email address, the dates you want, how many people are coming, and what you are camping in. If you email us, we hold the contents of that email.

There are no forms on this website. Nothing is collected from you unless you choose to ring or email us.

3. Why we collect it

To confirm availability, to hold your site, to contact you if something changes such as the camp closing for the season, and to keep basic records of who is staying on the property.

4. Who we share it with

Nobody, other than where we are required to by law or where it is genuinely needed in an emergency, for example passing details to emergency services. We do not sell or trade camper details, and we do not add anyone to a marketing list without being asked to.

5. How long we keep it

Camper details are kept only as long as we need them for the season and for our own records, then deleted.

6. This website

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Some parts of the page load from third party services, such as Google Fonts, the embedded Google Map and images hosted by Unsplash. Those services may log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser, under their own privacy policies. Links out to Google, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok take you to those platforms, which handle your information under their own terms.

7. Reviews

Reviews shown on this site were left publicly by campers on third party camping and mapping platforms. We do not publish camper names or details from private correspondence.

8. Your rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Ring Mandy on 021 223 1194 or use the email button on the contact section of the home page and we will sort it out.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
